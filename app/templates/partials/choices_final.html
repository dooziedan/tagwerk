{# Renaming final tracks (wizard step "Final tracks"; Settings → Final tracks). #}
Rename tracks when you mark them as final?

"Final" is a seal for tracks you've checked completely. Until then, Tagwerk never renames a file: writing tags and importing keep the name it arrived with. You mark tracks on the Final check page.
