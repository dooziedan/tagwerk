How Tagwerk checks ReplayGain
  • Measured: the integrated loudness of the whole track (EBU R128, like rsgain and foobar2000) and its true peak, with ffmpeg. Writing tags doesn't change the sound, so a track is measured once.
  • The gain it should have: {{ reference | int }} LUFS minus its loudness. A track at −7 LUFS gets −11.00 dB.
  • Matches when the tag is within half a dB: meters differ by a tenth or two, and nobody hears half a dB.
  • Old ReplayGain 1 value: about 4 dB higher than measured. Older taggers used a louder reference (89 dB), so these tracks play louder than the rest.
  • Album gain isn't changed: for DJ tracks, set Navidrome's ReplayGain mode to track.
